Good to know

FAQ

Is it vegetarian?

Yes, every dinner is fully vegetarian. We use organic animal products as much as possible.

What does it cost?

We keep it low-threshold. Check the ticket page for the current edition's contribution.

Do I need to bring anything?

Just yourself, an appetite for good food and a mood for good company.

Can I come alone?

Absolutely, in fact we encourage it.

I have an allergy or dietary need. Can I still come?

Yes, just let us know in advance. Email us with your allergy or dietary need and we'll do our best to take it into account.
