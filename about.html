About us

Where the cityeats together

Community Kitchen Amersfoort is a grassroots neighbourhood dinner, organised for and by fellow city residents. Every edition we serve a high-quality vegetarian dinner, made with rescued regional produce.

We believe a good meal is the easiest way to bring people together.

Connection is at the heart of Community Kitchen Amersfoort. At every dinner we create a setting where you get to know new people in a low-threshold way. We also team up with other local organisations each edition. By drawing on each other's strengths and qualities, we create a broad impact and a stronger local network.

Every edition we also work with a wonderful team of volunteers. Sometimes they're close friends, sometimes people who love the concept and simply want to be part of it. One is a professional cook, another mostly cooks at home. In practice it doesn't matter, everyone is welcome! Several times a year we host separate, smaller dinners for everyone who actively helps out, to make it extra special for our team.

And maybe the best part: we travel through the city. Because we don't have a fixed spot, we visit locations all across Amersfoort. That way we get to know many different people and sides of Amersfoort, and connect them with each other.
